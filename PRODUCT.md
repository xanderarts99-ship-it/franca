# Product

## Register

product

## Users and purpose
Rammies Vacation guests book ten vacation homes. Franca, the nontechnical owner, manages properties, prices, availability, bookings and reviews through the admin dashboard. Minimum stays must be editable without code or deployments.

## Design principles
- Keep the existing site's warm palette, typography and navigation conventions.
- Use everyday words and explicit labels; show the consequence of a rule before saving.
- Put usual settings first; reveal property exceptions and seasonal complexity only when needed.
- Make errors recoverable, preserve entered data, and show successful saves clearly.
- Use labelled native controls, keyboard focus, readable contrast and mobile-friendly layouts.

## Voice
Clear, calm and practical. Avoid technical policy terminology in owner-facing controls.
